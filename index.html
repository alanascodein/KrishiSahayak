How the cooperative loop works

2️⃣

✨ Main AI Feature

Photo-Based Crop Reports

This is the heart of KrishiSahayak. No forms, no jargon — just a photo. The AI describes what it can see (crop, affected part, symptoms, severity). It never guesses a disease name. You always confirm before saving, and reports stay unverified until an officer reviews them.

  • 📷 Take or choose a photo of the affected plant
  • 🤖 AI reads it in seconds — on the server, your key is safe
  • ✅ You check the pre-filled report and fix anything wrong
  • 🚨 Similar reports near you trigger a community risk signal
    • 📸 Try it now Sign in first

    🔒 Your photo's GPS location is removed automatically. Only your panchayat/locality is stored.

    Why cooperation beats working alone

    Farmers face the same problems in isolation. Machinery, irrigation, seeds, pests — the needs are shared, but the information isn't.
